# Global Time Off & Holidays Policy

This policy explains how **Globomantics** manages paid time off (PTO), public holidays, sick leave, and related practices across locations. It is a company policy (not legal advice). Local laws or works agreements may provide additional rights; when in doubt, the **more generous** rule applies.

---

## 1) Country Summary (Company-Provided Time Off)

The table below summarizes *company-provided* annual leave, sick leave, and holidays. Local nuances follow.

| Country | Annual Leave (company standard) | Company Holidays Observed | Floating Holidays | Company-Paid Sick Leave | Notes |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| United States (USA) | **20 days** | **10 days** | **2 days** | **10 days** | PTO combines vacation + personal days. State/local holidays vary; company holiday list below applies. |
| United Kingdom (UK) | **25 days** | **8 days** | **1 day** | **10 days** | Annual leave is in addition to UK bank holidays. Sickness certification required >7 calendar days. |
| Australia (AU) | **20 days** | **10 days** | **1 day** | **10 days** (personal/carer) | Public holidays vary by state/territory; company honors the nationally observed set plus local substitutions. |
| Germany (DE) | **30 days** | **10 days** (varies by state) | **0 days** | Up to **6 weeks** full pay per illness episode | Company standard annual leave is 30 days; federal holidays differ by state (Bundesland). Medical note required after 3 calendar days. |

> **Floating Holidays** let employees observe a cultural/religious/heritage day not on the local calendar. Unused floating days do not carry over.

## 3) Accrual, Carryover, and Blackout Periods

### Accrual
- **USA & AU:** PTO accrues each pay period. New hires receive a prorated grant.  
- **UK & DE:** Annual leave is granted upfront each holiday year and tracked in hours for partial-day usage.

### Carryover
- **Default:** Carry over up to **5 days** into the next holiday year; must be used by **March 31**.  
- **Germany:** Up to **5 days** carryover allowed until **March 31**; statutory carry rules apply when leave wasn’t possible due to illness or parental leave.  
- **Exceptional Carry:** Additional carry requires VP+ approval and a specific business need.

### Blackout Periods
- Customer-critical teams (e.g., Support, SRE) may publish **blackout windows** of up to 10 business days per quarter. Management must ensure equitable rotation of coverage.

## 4) Sick Leave & Medical Documentation

- **Short Illness (1–3 days):** No documentation required; inform your manager and set an OOO status.  
- **Extended Illness:**  
  - **USA/AU/UK:** Provide a medical note starting day 4.  
  - **DE:** Provide a medical note starting day 4 (earlier if requested by the manager based on pattern).  
- **Pay:** Company-paid sick leave per the country summary. In DE, the company pays up to 6 weeks per illness episode; thereafter statutory insurer rules may apply.

---

## 5) Requesting Time Off

1. Submit the request in the HRIS (select **PTO**, **Sick**, **Floating Holiday**, **Public Holiday Substitution**, or **Unpaid**).  
2. Add coverage notes (who will monitor tickets, on-call swaps, etc.).  
3. Manager approves or requests changes within **3 business days**.  
4. Block your calendar and update Slack/Teams status with dates and backup contact.
5. For **public holiday substitutions** (e.g., substituting a local religious festival), attach a brief note; approval is not withheld based on reason.

---

## 6) Public Holiday Substitution

Employees whose cultural/religious observances are **not** on the local calendar may:  
- Use **Floating Holidays** (where available), or  
- Request a **Public Holiday Substitution** (swap a listed company holiday for the desired observance).

> Example: A US employee may work on Veterans Day and take Diwali instead.

---

## 7) Regional Nuances (Quick Reference)

- **USA:** PTO is unified; regional school-closure calendars do not automatically grant leave. Jury Duty is paid (see Leave Types Policy).  
- **UK:** Annual leave is in addition to UK bank holidays. Part-time employees receive pro-rated bank holidays.  
- **Australia:** Some states observe additional public holidays (e.g., AFL Grand Final in VIC). We mirror the local calendar of the employee’s **primary work location**.  
- **Germany:** Vacation planning should consider Betriebsferien (site shutdowns) where announced.

---

## 8) Examples & Scenarios

- **Scenario A (US):** You want to observe Lunar New Year. Submit a **Public Holiday Substitution** (work Presidents’ Day, observe Lunar New Year).  
- **Scenario B (UK):** You’re part-time (60%). Bank holidays are pro-rated; the HRIS shows the pro-rated entitlement.  
- **Scenario C (DE):** You fall ill during approved vacation. Convert overlapping days to **Sick Leave** with a medical note and rebook your annual leave.

---

## 9) Definitions

- **Annual Leave/PTO:** Paid time away from work for rest, personal business, or vacation.  
- **Floating Holiday:** A paid day off of the employee’s choosing for observances not reflected in the local calendar.  
- **Public Holiday Substitution:** Swapping a listed company holiday with a different date of significance.  
- **Carryover:** Unused annual leave moved to the next holiday year.
