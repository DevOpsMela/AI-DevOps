# Leave Types & Procedures

This policy describes paid/unpaid leaves and how to request them. See the **Global Time Off & Holidays** policy for annual leave and holiday calendars.

---

## 1) Leave Types Overview

| Leave Type | Paid? | Standard Duration | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Annual Leave / PTO | Yes | Per country policy | Book in HRIS; see carryover rules. |
| Sick Leave | Yes | See country summary | Medical note required after 3 days. |
| Parental Leave (Primary Caregiver) | Yes | **16 weeks** | 100% pay; must be taken within 12 months of birth/placement. |
| Parental Leave (Secondary Caregiver) | Yes | **6 weeks** | 100% pay; can be split into 2 blocks. |
| Bereavement Leave | Yes | **5 days** | Up to 10 days for immediate family if travel required. |
| Jury Duty / Court Service | Yes | As required | Provide summons documentation. |
| Military Reserve / Emergency Services | Yes | As required | Provide orders; coordinate with manager. |
| Compassionate / Carer Leave | Yes | **5 days** | For urgent family care needs. |
| Volunteer Day | Yes | **1 day/year** | Pre-approved organizations. |
| Unpaid Leave | No | Up to **30 days** | Manager + People Ops approval. |

---

## 2) Country-Specific Notes (Days Off & Processes)

### United States (USA)
- **Annual Leave:** 20 days company standard.  
- **Company Holidays:** 10 per the holiday list.  
- **Sick Leave:** 10 company-paid days; state/local entitlements may be more generous.  
- **Parental Leave:** Company policy above; state programs may supplement.  
- **Jury Duty:** Paid for the service period; submit proof.

### United Kingdom (UK)
- **Annual Leave:** 25 days plus 8 bank holidays.  
- **Sick Leave:** 10 company-paid days; statutory sick pay rules may also apply. Fit note after 7 calendar days.  
- **Parental Leave:** Company policy above; Shared Parental Leave supported with HR assistance.

### Australia (AU)
- **Annual Leave:** 20 days; shift-workers may have additional entitlements.  
- **Public Holidays:** 10 baseline; state substitutions apply.  
- **Personal/Carer’s Leave (Sick):** 10 days company-paid.  
- **Parental Leave:** Company policy above; coordinate with Services Australia if applicable.

### Germany (DE)
- **Annual Leave:** 30 days.  
- **Public Holidays:** Approx. 10; state variations.  
- **Sick Leave:** Company pays up to 6 weeks per illness episode; medical certificate required from day 4.  
- **Parental Leave:** Company policy above; Elterngeld (state program) may apply separately.

---

## 3) How to Request Leave

1. **Start in HRIS:** Choose the specific leave type.  
2. **Attach Documentation:** Medical notes, birth/adoption certificates, travel proof for bereavement as applicable.  
3. **Discuss Coverage:** Coordinate handoffs, on-call swaps, and customer escalations.  
4. **Approval Timelines:**  
   - Annual/Sick: manager decision within 3 business days.  
   - Parental/Bereavement: prioritized within 2 business days.  
5. **Confirm:** Calendar block + Slack OOO + delegation in email/ITSM.

---

## 4) Accrual & Pay Treatment

- Annual leave accrues/grants per **Global Time Off** policy.  
- Parental leave is paid at **100% base salary**; variable pay pauses unless contractually guaranteed.  
- Sick leave is paid per country rules; extended illness may transition to local statutory programs or disability plans.

---

## 5) Special Cases

- **Illness during Vacation:** Convert overlapping days to Sick Leave with documentation.  
- **Public Holiday during Leave:** If a company holiday falls during Annual Leave, it does **not** count against Annual Leave in the UK/DE; for USA/AU, follow local practice (company does not deduct).  
- **Part-Time Employees:** Leave entitlements are **pro-rated** by scheduled hours.  
- **Probationary Period:** New hires can use leave; approvals consider team coverage and training needs.

---

## 6) Examples

- **US parental leave:** Primary caregiver takes 10 weeks after birth and 6 weeks later—approved; must complete within 12 months.  
- **DE sick leave:** Employee ill for 8 weeks—first 6 weeks company-paid; thereafter statutory insurer pay may apply.  
- **AU bereavement travel:** Additional 5 days granted due to international travel.
